# CivicClear: AI for clearer reports for homelessness safety

Client: City of San José, homelessness safety and community reporting  
Team: Lola Bui, Sri Sudula, Juan Chavez, Benjamin Lok, Aug–Oct 2026  

My part: I helped define the CivicClear reporting, researched the resident-reporting problem, defined the system’s inputs and outputs, identified structured extraction as the main AI capability, helped create the human-review and safety process, revised four AI Canvas fields after feedback, tested CivicClear’s failure case, helped add contradiction detection, and contributed to the CivicClear creation in Replit.

## The problem

People experiencing homelessness and other San José residents may have difficulty reporting safety or sidewalk concerns to the correct City department. They may also be unsure whether their report was reviewed or what will happen next.

## What we built

- CivicClear accepts a resident’s report about a sidewalk or public-safety concern.
- The AI organizes the report, identifies missing or conflicting details, and creates a draft for City staff to review.
- A City employee still makes the final decision, checks urgent information, and decides whether the report should be routed or acted on.

## What I would do next

One gap is that CivicClear is not connected to verified City systems. Next, I would build secure storage and validated City-department routing while keeping human review before any final action.
