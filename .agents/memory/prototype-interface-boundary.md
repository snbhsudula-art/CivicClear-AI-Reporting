name: Prototype interface boundary
description: Mobile-first resident and detailed staff UX, class-prototype scope, and why view separation is not authorization.
---

Keep resident and staff experiences distinct in navigation, wording, and available controls, but describe switching between them as a demo workflow, not secure role separation.

**Why:** The user explicitly limited CivicClear to a class prototype and prohibited adding a database, authentication system, or real City integrations for this interface work. Visual separation must not imply privacy or authorization guarantees.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing safety and human-review gates when changing presentation. Do not expand this into an account system or City service unless the user explicitly changes the scope.

Prioritize a mobile-first, app-like resident experience while keeping detailed AI analysis and decision controls in the separate desktop/tablet staff workspace.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a simple, one-handed civic reporting experience rather than a desktop website squeezed onto a phone, without losing evidence or staff review features.

**How to apply:** Keep resident steps and tracking clear and touch-friendly. Simplify presentation through disclosures, not by deleting safety warnings, missing-information needs, original evidence, or human-review safeguards.
